Privacy Policy for NumFlow

Effective Date: August 31, 2026

Thank you for playing NumFlow ("we", "our", or "us"). Your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use the NumFlow mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

NumFlow is built as an offline-first educational math game. We do not collect, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your real name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

  • Local Game Progress & Preferences: Level progress (Levels 1–20), question statistics, audio/sound preferences, and haptic vibration settings are stored locally on your device via Android DataStore.
  • Advertising & Analytics: We integrate Google AdMob to deliver banner and rewarded video advertisements. Google AdMob may automatically process non-identifying device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID, IP address for general geolocation, and performance diagnostics) in compliance with Google's Privacy Policy.

2. Data Control & Game Reset

You have total control over your local data. You can clear or reset all your game progression back to Level 1 at any time using the Reset Game Progress option in the app Settings menu.

3. Third-Party Services

NumFlow uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify device characteristics:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob Privacy Policy

4. Data Security

Because your game data is stored locally on your Android device, it remains under your direct control. We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to any third party.

5. Children's Privacy & Safety

NumFlow is a family-friendly educational math puzzle game designed for players of all ages. We comply with all applicable child protection laws (including COPPA and Google Play Families Policy) and do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be updated on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at support@numflow.com.
